CARE CENTER

Simple care. Healthier plants.

Follow these basic guidelines to help your plants grow healthy.

01

Watering

Check the soil before watering. Avoid keeping soil unnecessarily wet.

02

Sunlight

Give each plant the amount of light suitable for its specific requirements.

03

Soil

Use suitable, well-draining soil and select the right pot size.

04

Fertilizer

Use appropriate fertilizer according to the plant's growth requirements.

05

Pruning

Remove damaged or dead parts carefully to support healthy growth.

06

Repotting

Repot when the plant outgrows its container or the soil becomes unsuitable.

SEASONAL GUIDE

Care changes with the season.

☀️
SUMMER

Protect from heat

Monitor soil moisture, protect sensitive plants from extreme heat and provide appropriate shade where needed.

🌧️
MONSOON

Prevent waterlogging

Ensure drainage, avoid unnecessary watering and watch for fungal problems.

❄️
WINTER

Reduce watering

Monitor soil moisture and protect sensitive plants from very low temperatures.
